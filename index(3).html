The curriculum

From language foundations to job interviews.

Nine learning modules designed to build practical English skills in a clear, step-by-step sequence.

MODULE 01🔊

Sounds of English

Pronunciation foundations, spelling on the phone, numbers, difficult sounds, word endings, and stress.

MODULE 02✍️

Grammar Essentials

Core tenses, modals, polite requests, questions, conditionals, and linking words for clearer answers.

MODULE 03📘

Word Power

Customer service and sales vocabulary, useful collocations, phrasal verbs, and positive language.

MODULE 04☎️

Telephone English

The seven steps of a good call, call phrases, understanding accents, and professional voice skills.

MODULE 05🤝

Customer Service Skills

Empathy, apologies, complaint handling, polite refusals, and de-escalation techniques.

MODULE 06📈

Sales English

Discovery questions, product features and benefits, handling objections, and closing a sale.

MODULE 07✉️

Writing for Work

Email structure, chat support English, punctuation, capital letters, and professional messages.

MODULE 08🎙️

Fluency & Pronunciation Lab

Sentence stress, rhythm, intonation, connected speech, chunking, and a daily speaking routine.

MODULE 09🎯

Interview Training

125 questions and sample answers, interview strategy, mock interviews, scoring, and checklists.

Built for active practice

Don't just study English. Use English.

The workbook encourages consistent practice, speaking aloud, recording your voice, reviewing mistakes, and tracking your progress.
